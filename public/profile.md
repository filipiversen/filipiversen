## How I work

### Keep the work in context

Keep the information people need beside the task they’re doing, with room to move when the work isn’t linear.

- [Lending workspace](https://www.filipiversen.com/projects/lenders-cooperative#a-sequence-that-leaves-room-to-move)
- [Survey Loop editor](https://www.filipiversen.com/projects/survey-loop#from-a-form-to-a-canvas)

### Design the uncertain states

Make it clear what has saved, what failed, and what needs a person’s approval. Give people a way to recover.

- [Save states](https://www.filipiversen.com/projects/survey-loop#saving-is-part-of-the-interaction)
- [Policy recovery](https://www.filipiversen.com/projects/insurance-policy-platform#recovering-an-accepted-policy-submission)
- [Estimate approval](https://www.filipiversen.com/projects/eret#reviewing-an-assistants-changes)

### Carry decisions into implementation

Work through the details in tokens, components, and frontend behavior, alongside the people building the product.

- [Peak, from Figma to code](https://www.filipiversen.com/projects/lenders-cooperative#peak-from-figma-to-the-product)
- [Survey Loop’s component library](https://www.filipiversen.com/writing/build-table-components-like-lego)
